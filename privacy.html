Privacy, plainly stated.

What this frontend demo stores and why.

Local browser data

This demo stores account details, cart items, wishlist items, reviews, addresses and orders in your browser’s localStorage. The data is not sent to a Shopora Mini Mart server.

External services

Product images are loaded from Unsplash. Tracking links open Google Maps. Those services may apply their own privacy policies.

Your control

You can clear local data through your browser settings or log out from your Shopora Mini Mart account.
