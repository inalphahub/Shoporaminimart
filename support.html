Questions, answered.

Find quick answers about delivery, returns, payments and your Shopora Mini Mart account.

How can I track my order?

Open My account → My orders and select Track on Maps. You’ll also see the latest order status there.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders can be cancelled from My account → My orders while they are still being prepared.

What is the return window?

Most products are eligible for easy returns within 7 days of delivery.

Is checkout secure?

This demo includes a secure checkout experience ready to connect to your preferred payment provider.
