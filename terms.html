Using Shopora Mini Mart

Shopora Mini Mart is a frontend demonstration storefront. Product listings, prices, account records and orders are simulated in your browser for evaluation and prototyping.

Orders and payments

Checkout does not process real payments. Any order created in this demo is stored locally and can be cancelled from your account.

Content and availability

Product imagery and descriptions are demo content. Availability, prices and delivery estimates are illustrative and may change.
